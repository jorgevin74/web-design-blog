Discussion:

In the first reading, I think it is important to have that sort of preservation of creativity because as the author mentioned with the increase of companies such as SquareSpace and Wix and the normalization of A.I use at workplaces. We'd lose our creative spark and then, every single website would have the exact same layout and feel to it that doesn't come off as genuine or natural. I didn't know about Wix firing their employee for their pro palestine comments. I think that was pretty shitty of them to do that, and I am pretty glad myself I never caved in to get one of those Wix / Squarespace subscriptions or even try to use their website because of that. I do see how building websites and hosting them on servers, aren't just that, they are but it's as the author put it "Ethical, Political and Philosophical" and I think it is important to check and hold companies like Wix accountable and better to practice doing stuff on our own rather than relying on companies and A.I to do the work for us.

I never knew that Roblox was that deep for people developing games in there. I knew that Roblox was a big hub for developers who are into game design or want to create a space of their own have and provide that for users that are actually interested in it. I know other games are following that same idea, one of them being Fortnite where it is taking a Roblox approach and while it has its standard gamemodes such as Battle Royale and LEGO and such, It does have a huge hub of games and lobbies where people can hang out with their skins and could be Leon from Resident Evil or V from Cyberpunk and run around in a digital world. I do think that by making worlds in these kinds of games we are leaving a digital footprint, telling the community that this is what I made and what I want others, or maybe for myself to experience and I think that making a game as big and popular is very interesting considering that they are planning to make a movie based off one of Roblox's popular games called "Grow A Garden" & "99 Nights In The Forest".

I do think that we are heading into a dead internet theory sort of space. I remember recently I watched a youtube video of content creator playing a video game that I liked and it was weird going into the comments and like two of the top comments were very clearly bot comments and the people replying to the bot comment were just as confused. Luckily the rest were regular people but there are bots here and there especially in big youtube videos. I do miss the feeling of opening up the computer when I was younger and just having fun in different websites and games that I remember fodnly of. There's something similar that the article mentions about having those spaces that I sort of relate to, I used to play this online game called Marvel Super Hero Squad Online, and it was a free game you could download and play all you needed was to sign up and you could join and be in a hub with thousands of other players and do quests along the city, interact with others, chat and even go on missions. It was a really fun experience since I love Marvel Comics and seeing the characters come to life in a cartoonish friendly manner was awesome. Sadly that game got discontinued and isn't available anymore. However! I did recently look it up again and noticed that there is like a fan-made version of the Super Hero Squad Online Game which will be linked Here and if you go around it is purely made with HTML, CSS and JS to run the site up. I do think it is interesting how even though a game or a site can shut down, if it had tons of fans or a big following, they'll come up with ways to get that back and it is kind of incredible and nostalgic even for me so I'll try it out this weekened to truly see if it is the exact same as I remember.
